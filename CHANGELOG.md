## v0.2.0

- **Braun / Apple Industrial Light Aesthetic**: Redesigned the entire interface in a crisp Apple + Dieter Rams light theme (`#F5F5F7` alabaster canvas, `#FFFFFF` cards, precision `#ECECEF` anodized dials, and `#FF5500` International Orange accents).
- **Ergonomic Dual-Thumb Semi-Circular Dials & Horizontal Rollers**: Positioned left (`HOUR 00-23`) and right (`MIN 00-59`) semi-circular rotary wheels in the lower thumb zone alongside recessed horizontal mechanical rollers for repeat and holiday rules.
- **Built-in Calendar & Events Manager**: Interactive monthly calendar grid with `OFF` / `WORK` / `PTO` badges, horizontal roller filter, and full event CRUD.
- **External `.ics` Subscriptions & iOS `EventKit` Calendar Access**: Dedicated settings screen for managing on-device `EventKit` calendar access, PTO keywords, external `.ics` holiday feeds, and Keychain-backed BYOK AI parsing.
